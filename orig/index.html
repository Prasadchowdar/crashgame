HOW TO PLAY

Be the few. Take the pool.

  1. 1

    Pick a stake

    0.10 to 5.00 demo credits. Change it any time before picks close.

  2. 2

    Tap a door

    Red, Gold or Blue. A crowd of about 1,000 players floods in live. You can switch doors every 1 second.

  3. 3

    The lock drops

    At a hidden moment between 10s and 20s the doors slam shut. Picks close a split second earlier, while the crowd makes its final swing.

  4. 4

    Smallest door wins

    The door with the fewest players bursts open and pays everyone inside. The smaller the door, the bigger the multiplier.

  • Tie between the two smallest doors: payout is split in half.
  • All three doors exactly equal: your stake is refunded.
  • Max payout is capped at 100 credits per round.
  • House Mode: the crowd is simulated. Its final split is sealed (hashed) before the round, so nothing you do can change it. Tap Verify after a round to check.
  • Prototype with demo credits only. No real money.

MATH & RTP

97.0%measured RTP · 30,000,000 simulated rounds
  • The final split is drawn from the round seed, from a mix of balanced, lopsided and stampede crowds, and is symmetric across doors. So RTP is the same whichever door you pick.
  • Picks close 0.6s before the lock, while the crowd makes its final swing, so late-crowd reading can't beat the math.
  • Ties (two smallest doors equal): half payout each. Observed in 0.79% of rounds. Three-way tie: refund.
  • Max payout per round: 100 credits = 25× the max stake, so the cap never reduces any payout and the RTP holds at every stake.
  • Crowd size N = 1,000 (the player is not counted in it).

PROVABLY FAIR

Before each round a SHA-256 hash of lockTime | seed | finalSplit is published. After it, the seed is revealed.
