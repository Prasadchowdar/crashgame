FULL RULES

Be the few. Take the pool.

  1. 1

    Pick your bet

    Tap a chip: 0.10 to 5.00 demo credits.

  2. 2

    Tap a door

    Red, Gold or Blue. The crowd floods in live. You can switch doors once every second.

  3. 3

    The lock drops

    At a hidden moment between 10s and 20s (the striped part of the bar) the doors slam shut. Bets close a split second earlier.

  4. 4

    Fewest people wins

    The door with the fewest people opens and pays everyone inside. The emptier it is, the bigger the win. The number above each door shows what you win if that door ends up emptiest.

  • Tie between the two emptiest doors: the win is split in half.
  • All three doors exactly equal: your bet comes back.
  • Max win is 125 credits per round.
  • House Mode: the crowd is simulated. Its final split is sealed (hashed) before the round, so nothing anyone does can change it. Tap FAIR to check any round.
  • Prototype with demo credits only. No real money.

MATH & RTP

97.0%measured RTP · 30,000,000 simulated rounds
  • The final split is drawn from the round seed, from a mix of balanced, lopsided and stampede crowds, and is symmetric across doors. So RTP is the same whichever door you pick.
  • Bets close 0.6s before the lock, while the crowd makes its final swing, so late-crowd reading can't beat the math.
  • Ties (two emptiest doors equal): half win each. Observed in 0.79% of rounds. Three-way tie: refund.
  • Max win per round: 125 credits = 25× the max bet, so the cap never reduces any win.
  • Crowd size N = 1,000 (the player is not counted in it).

PROVABLY FAIR

Before each round a SHA-256 hash of lockTime | seed | finalSplit is published. After it, the seed is revealed.
